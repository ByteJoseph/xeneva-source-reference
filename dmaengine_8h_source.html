4/*
5 * DCL <linux/dmaengine.h> -- the four names struct uart_8250_dma spells out,
6 * and nothing else.
7 *
8 * mainline's copy is 56 KB: the dma_device/dma_chan objects, the channel
9 * request/release and slave-capability machinery, cyclic descriptors,
10 * interleaved transfers, the whole engine. Xeneva has no DMA layer, and 8250
11 * on every board in scope (iMX8MP, RPi3b+, QEMU's pl011/16550) runs PIO.
12 * What the driver does need is for the *struct* to parse, because
13 * 8250.h:20-44 embeds two dma_filter_fn, two dma_slave_config and two
14 * dma_cookie_t as fields of uart_8250_dma -- fields whose values only matter
15 * if CONFIG_SERIAL_8250_DMA code is compiled in, which it is not.
16 *
17 * So the four declarations below are copied from mainline rather than
18 * reduced, for the reason <linux/serial_core.h>'s header comment gives: a
19 * struct that disagrees with its vendor source by one field compiles cleanly
20 * and then reads the wrong offset, and a type whose layout is invented here
21 * is the same trap in a smaller box. The rest of the engine is absent because
22 * nothing calls it -- see the same rule at the top of <linux/cleanup.h>.
23 *
24 * upstream include/linux/dmaengine.h (mainline v7.2)
25 * extracted: enum dma_transfer_direction, enum dma_slave_buswidth,
26 * dma_cookie_t, dma_filter_fn, struct dma_slave_config
27 */
