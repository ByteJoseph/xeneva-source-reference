Detailed Description

struct qaic_wait - Defines a blocking wait for BO execution. @handle: In. GEM handle of the BO to wait on. @timeout: In. Maximum time in ms to wait for the BO. @dbc_id: In. DBC the BO is submitted to. @pad: Structure padding. Must be 0.
