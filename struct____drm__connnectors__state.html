struct drm_connector_state* __drm_connnectors_state::state_to_destroy

@state_to_destroy:

Used to track the @drm_connector_state we will need to free when tearing down the associated &drm_atomic_commit in $drm_mode_config_funcs.atomic_state_clear or drm_atomic_commit_default_clear().

Before a commit, and the call to drm_atomic_helper_swap_state() in particular, it points to the same state than @new_state. After a commit, it points to the same state than @old_state.
