9 * How this file was produced (python3 /tmp/mkbasehdr.py):
10 * - upstream text is copied as-is; these are layout-critical declarations
11 * and a hand-typed `struct uart_port` that disagrees with the vendor
12 * sources by one field would compile cleanly and then read the wrong
13 * offset at runtime, which is exactly the class of bug this whole
14 * exercise exists to avoid
15 * - <uapi/linux/X> content is inlined in place of its include: DCL has no
16 * uapi tree, BaseHdr is flat, and no vendored .c names a uapi path
17 * - the driver-local headers (8250.h, serial_base.h,
18 * serial_mctrl_gpio.h) are NOT here -- they are private to their driver
19 * and live in Vendored/ beside the .c that includes them, resolved by
20 * quoted-include rules
21 *
22 * Everything below this comment is mainline's. DCL-specific deviations are
