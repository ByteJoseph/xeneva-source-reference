1/*
2 * DCL tracepoint.h -- TRACE_EVENT becomes an empty static inline.
3 *
4 * drm_trace.h and trace/events/dma_fence.h declare events the drm/dma-fence
5 * .c files emit at runtime. The declarations keep the call sites parsing;
6 * the bodies do nothing (Xeneva has no ftrace). CREATE_TRACE_POINTS and
7 * DEFINE_TRACE/EXPORT_TRACEPOINT_SYMBOL expand to nothing, so the
8 * define_trace.h include at the bottom of those headers is inert too.
9 *
10 * TP_PROTO parenthesizes and the three generators do *not* add a second
11 * pair: `static inline void trace_##name proto { }` with proto expanding to
12 * `(struct dma_fence *fence)` is the declaration. Adding parens around
13 * `proto` as well produced `trace_fence_signaled((struct dma_fence *fence))`
14 * -- a parameter *expression* in parentheses, which has no name and so no
15 * type, and clang reported every one of trace/events/dma_fence.h's seven
16 * events as "type specifier missing ... implicit int".
17 *
18 * Each generator emits the call stub *and* mainline's `_enabled()` companion
19 * (tracepoint.h:222, which mainline builds on static_key_false). Every
20 * call site is `if (trace_x_enabled()) trace_x(...)`, and without the
21 * companion the condition is an implicit call at link time -- dma-fence.c:555
22 * is the one that did it, `trace_dma_fence_wait_end_enabled()` inside
23 * dma_fence_wait_timeout(). `false` rather than a static key: there is no
24 * ftrace to flip it on, which is the same reason the bodies are empty.
25 */
