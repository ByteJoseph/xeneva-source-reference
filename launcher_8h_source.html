30/*
31 * launcher.h -- XELnch internal header.
32 *
33 * The start panel is a magazine spread on gamusa cotton:
34 *
35 * ==== pari =========================================================
36 * seek [o][r] 1:04
37 * type to find an app ____ thursday, 5 november
38 * always here ----------------------- controls . xeneva . all apps >
39 * <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <> <>
40 * [ AHEntryList: bento spread of cards, justified rows ]
41 * [ tall row: hero . files . glimpse . calendar ]
42 * [ short row: notes . netsurf . playverse . doom . ... ]
43 * [=] your day . 3 new
44 * ~~~~~~~~~~~~ LuitFlow: the day as a ribbon, a pointer you scrub ~~~
45 * ==== phul band ====================================================
46 *
47 * Memory (bounded, freed eagerly):
48 * - window: the canvas draws straight into the compositor's buffer
49 * (no second full-panel copy)
50 * - text: every string is drawn from a few pre-rasterised glyph sets
51 * (8-bit coverage, ~150 KB in all); no font is touched after start-up,
52 * so there is never a glyph-atlas re-bake while painting
53 * - icons: one small (list / plate) per app, one big only while pinned
54 * - LuitFlow: a fixed ring of XL_FLOW_MAX items
55 *
56 * Time:
57 * - card hit test: binary search over rows, then over the cards of the
58 * row, O(log n); list rows O(1)
59 * - app lookup by hash: O(log n)
60 * - bento layout: O(n), only when pins / order change
61 * - painting: O(pixels of the dirty rect); a hover change repaints only
62 * the previous and the current item
63 */
