#define TEL_IAC   255

Telnet client for XenevaOS (terminal app).

Usage: telnet <host> [port] (default port 23, host:port accepted)

Minimal NVT: replies WONT to every DO and DONT to every WILL, except SGA (we WILL it, we already send char-at-a-time) and TTYPE (we WILL it and answer "VT100", matching the Xeneva Terminal's ANSI/VT100 support). Ctrl+] quits. Close detection relies on AuTCPReceive returning 0 on FIN/close and -1 when the RX queue is empty.
