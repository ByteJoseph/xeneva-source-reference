159/*
160 * DCL delta -- `uintptr_t`, not mainline's `unsigned long`.
161 *
162 * arg is the caller's ioctl pointer (DRM_IOCTL_VERSION passes the address
163 * of its struct drm_version, for example). This target is
164 * aarch64-unknown-windows, which is LLP64 (sizeof(long) == 4), so
165 * mainline's spelling would carry only the low half of the address; apps
166 * here link at 0x600000000. The vendored definition in
167 * drivers/gpu/drm/drm_ioctl.c carries the same delta, and
168 * DCL/linux_cdev_shim.c's ioctl_fn typedef
169 * follows so the call does not narrow at the ABI boundary.
170 */
