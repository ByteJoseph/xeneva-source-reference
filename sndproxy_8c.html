sndproxy.c – kernel half of a user-space sound driver.

A server process (Process/sndsrv) that owns the sound hardware through /dev/hwdev passes the kernel a shared ring (see BaseHdr/sndsrv.h). The kernel then registers an ordinary sound card, "virtio-sound", whose write() copies the mixer's PCM into that ring instead of touching hardware. The rest of the sound stack (mixer, DSP clients) cannot tell the difference.

Safety rules:

  • the ring must be one of the caller's own hwdev DMA buffers (AuHwdevOwnsDma)
  • write() never spins or waits on the server: it paces itself by sleeping, and if the server stops draining, data is dropped rather than hanging
  • when the server's device handle goes away (exit/crash) the card is removed and in-flight writers are drained BEFORE its memory is freed
