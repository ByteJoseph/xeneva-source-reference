◆ AuSoundUnregisterCard()

AU_EXTERN AU_EXPORT int AuSoundUnregisterCard ( AuSound * snd)

AuSoundUnregisterCard – remove a sound card (hot-unplug); DSP clients bound to it are detached. Non-zero only if the card was not registered.

AuSoundUnregisterCard – remove a sound card (hot-unplug); DSP clients bound to it are detached. Non-zero only if the card was not registered.

Parameters
snd– pointer to sound card

AuSoundUnregisterCard – remove a card from the registry (hot-unplug). DSP clients bound to the card are detached (_cardID = -1) rather than the removal being refused: a client such as init holds its binding for the whole uptime, so refusing would make a sound driver permanently un-unloadable. Detached clients fall back to the software buffer path and must re-select a card after the driver is loaded again. Every user of _cards[] already tolerates an empty slot. Returns 0, or non-zero if snd was not registered.
