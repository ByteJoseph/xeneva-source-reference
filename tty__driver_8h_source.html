4/*
5 * DCL <linux/tty_driver.h> -- struct tty_driver and struct tty_operations.
6 *
7 * Ported from mainline v7.2, with two classes of change:
8 *
9 * - __randomize_layout dropped from both structs. It is a GCC plugin that
10 * shuffles field order per build to defeat struct-prediction attacks; DCL
11 * has no plugin and no adversary, and a struct whose layout differs between
12 * translation units would be a correctness bug, not a hardening feature.
13 *
14 * - The CONFIG_PROC_FS and CONFIG_CONSOLE_POLL blocks are written out in the
15 * "present" direction (proc registration is declared, the poll ops are
16 * not). proc_tty_register_driver() is one of the calls tty_register_driver()
17 * makes, so it needs a symbol; poll_* is a console-polling hook nothing in
18 * the staged set installs.
19 *
20 * `struct cdev **cdevs` is the field that makes /dev/ttyS0 possible at all:
21 * tty_io.c's cdev bridge looks the driver up through it, and serial_core.c's
22 * uart_add_one_port() eventually lands here. It is an array -- one cdev per
23 * line -- because a serial driver can have as many ports as minors.
24 */
