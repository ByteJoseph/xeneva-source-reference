Paper ash: a closing window crumbles from its top edge into small flakes. Each flake heats up (ember glow), breaks off, chars to grey ash, tumbles, shrinks, rises faster and faster with a sideways wobble and fades out. Flakes that have not broken off yet are the untouched window, so the first frame is identical to the window that was just closed.

The window's pixels are snapshotted by DeodhaiPaperAshStart, which the close handler calls before DeodhaiCloseWindow unmaps the shared memory; the close itself (and the client's DEODHAI_REPLY_WINDOW_CLOSED) is not delayed. Everything after that plays from the snapshot.

Shared by both compositors:

  • 2D: XRComposeFrame -> DeodhaiComposePaperAsh (main.cpp)
  • 3D: XRComposeFrame3D passes a projector; flakes stay on the window's plane (rising along its own "up"), with perspective

Cost per frame, P flakes:

  • O(P) motion, four flakes per NEON step (SoA arrays); flakes are kept sorted by break-off time (counting sort, O(P) once), so only the flakes in the air are touched, and per column the attached part is one pointer that only moves forward
  • O(flake pixels) to draw: every flake is an octagon whose spans are solved per row from 4 slab inequalities (no per-pixel inside test); texels are gathered with NEON index math, coloured (ember / char / fade) and blended four pixels per NEON step
  • repaint area via a 32 px tile bitmap (dirtytiles.h): marking is O(tiles touched), emitting rectangles is a bit scan, O(tiles / 64 + rectangles) – independent of how many flakes overlap

typedef bool(* PaperAshProjectFn) (const PaperAshPlane *plane, float lx, float ly, float lz, float *out)

Projects a point of the window plane: local (lx, ly) in window texels, lz along the plane normal (negative = towards the viewer). out[0..1] = screen position, out[2..5] = d(screen)/d(lx, ly) as {dsx/dlx, dsx/dly, dsy/dlx, dsy/dly}. false: behind the near plane.
