4/*
5 * DCL <linux/ioctl.h> -- the _IOC encoding, from uapi/asm-generic/ioctl.h.
6 *
7 * WHY THIS EXISTS ALONGSIDE BaseHdr/Fs/tty.h
8 * ------------------------------------------
9 * Xeneva's own devfs already has an ioctl vocabulary (Fs/tty.h: TIOCGWINSZ
10 * 0x5401 and friends), written as bare numbers because that is what Xeneva's
11 * dispatch compares against. The mainline tty stack does not work that way:
12 * several of its request numbers are *computed* from a direction/size/type
13 * tuple, and serial_core.c compares against two of them --
14 *
15 * case TIOCSISO7816: case TIOCGISO7816: (serial_core.c:1601,1605)
16 *
17 * -- which are _IOWR('T', 0x43, struct serial_iso7816) and
18 * _IOR('T', 0x42, struct serial_iso7816), i.e. they cannot be written as
19 * plain integers without baking in sizeof(struct serial_iso7816).
20 *
21 * So there are two encodings in the tree for now. That is a real
22 * inconsistency, not an oversight, and it is recorded as an open question in
23 * DCL_TODO.md for the stage that brings tty_io.c's dispatch over: whoever
24 * wires request routing has to decide which encoding userspace speaks. Until
25 * then this header is mainline's, unmodified in layout, so nothing that
26 * depends on the encoding is quietly wrong.
27 *
28 * upstream include/uapi/asm-generic/ioctl.h (mainline v7.2)
29 */
