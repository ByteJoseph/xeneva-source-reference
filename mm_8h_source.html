158/* ── struct vm_area_struct ───────────────────────────────────────────────
159 *
160 * mainline keeps this in <linux/mm_types.h>. DCL's cannot: the struct's
161 * vm_page_prot member is a pgprot_t, pgprot_t is defined at the top of this
162 * file, and mm_types.h is included *by* it -- the definition would have to
163 * run the other way. So it lives here, next to the type it needs.
164 *
165 * It is the whole struct and not just the members drm reads, because a
166 * partial struct is indistinguishable from a complete one until someone adds
167 * a member: the compiler would accept a write to a field that is not there
168 * and place it at whatever offset it had computed, which is the failure mode
169 * an ABI pin exists to prevent.

172 * drm_gem.c touches -- vm_start, vm_end, vm_flags, vm_page_prot, vm_pgoff,

174 * place and which nothing here reads yet. This is a layout nothing persists

196 * They are mainline's long-standing numbers, and they are spelled as numbers
197 * rather than as mainline v7.2 spells them -- v7.2 moved the entire VM_* set
198 * onto a VMA_*_BIT enum whose numbering is generated by a DECLARE_VMA_BIT()

229/*
230 * vma_pages() -- the length of a mapping in pages, from its bounds.
231 * drm_gem.c:1380 passes the result of this to drm_gem_object_lookup_at_offset()
232 * as the extent to search. Undefined, it was an implicit int-returning
233 * function, which is a warning -- the reason it is defined rather than left
