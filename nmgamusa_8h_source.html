30/*
31 * nmgamusa.h -- the Gamusa look for Namdapha (the default).
32 *
33 * Build with -DNAMDAPHA_CLASSIC to get the old glass bar back; both looks
34 * are compiled in and NmGamusa() picks one, so nothing else changes.
35 *
36 * The bar is cotton paper with a woven border: a red band and its light
37 * companion thread along the top, a row of small lozenges along the
38 * bottom. Running apps carry a sand lozenge, the focused one the red
39 * thread, the Go logo is dyed red. The bar is opaque, so it is created
40 * without WINDOW_FLAG_GLASS (no blur buffers in the compositor).
41 */
