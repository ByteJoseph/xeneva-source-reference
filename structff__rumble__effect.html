Detailed Description

struct ff_rumble_effect - defines parameters of a periodic force-feedback effect @strong_magnitude: magnitude of the heavy motor @weak_magnitude: magnitude of the light one

Some rumble pads have two motors of different weight. Strong_magnitude represents the magnitude of the vibration generated by the heavy one.
