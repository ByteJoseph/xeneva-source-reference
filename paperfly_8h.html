Paper flight: hiding a window rolls it up from its left edge into a paper tube that flies off to the right of the screen on a gentle arc; unhiding plays the same flight backwards – the tube flies in from the right and unrolls onto the window's place, and only then is the window shown again. Toggling mid-flight turns the paper around from where it is.

The module is shared by both compositors:

  • 2D: XRComposeFrame calls DeodhaiComposePaperFly (main.cpp), which repaints DeodhaiPaperFlyDirty() and draws with DeodhaiPaperFlyDraw()
  • 3D: XRComposeFrame3D applies the same per-column roll table through the window's own perspective mapping, so the paper rolls and flies in world space

The window is snapshotted when the flight starts, so a client closing mid-flight cannot pull the pixels away.

Cost per frame for one paper of w x h:

  • O(w) to rebuild the column table (roll geometry, lighting)
  • O(painted area) to draw: the flat part of each row is one NEON blend straight from the snapshot row, the tube (about 2 x radius columns) is gathered and shaded four pixels per NEON step
