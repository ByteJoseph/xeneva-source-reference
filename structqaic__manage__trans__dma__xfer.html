Detailed Description

struct qaic_manage_trans_dma_xfer - Defines a DMA transfer transaction. @hdr: In. Header to identify this transaction. @tag: In. Identified this transfer in other transactions. Opaque to the driver. @pad: Structure padding. @addr: In. Address of the data to DMA to the device. @size: In. Length of the data to DMA to the device.
