auipc.c – generic kernel IPC (see BaseHdr/auipc.h).

All state lives in two small fixed tables guarded by masking interrupts: the kernel is single-CPU here and no operation here blocks while holding them. A sleeping operation checks its condition and goes to sleep with interrupts masked, so a wake-up cannot slip in between (same rule as /dev/hwdev IRQ_WAIT).
