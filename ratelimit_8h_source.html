4/*
5 * DCL <linux/ratelimit.h> -- error printing that does not repeat itself.
6 *
7 * The staged set needs the *_ratelimited spellings (serial_core.c:1014 warns
8 * once per process about deprecated ASYNC_ flags; tty_io.c does the same for
9 * legacy TIOC* ioctls), and it includes this header to get them.
10 *
11 * DCL has no ratelimit_state worth the name -- there is no jiffies-driven
12 * suppression to borrow cheaply, and a suppressed warning on a single-user
13 * console is a warning nobody was going to act on anyway. So the macros pass
14 * straight through to their pr_* counterparts. The result is *louder* than
15 * mainline, never quieter, and that is the direction to be wrong in: a
16 * deprecation warning printed every time is noise; one silently dropped
17 * because a rate limiter ticked wrong is a lost diagnostic.
18 *
19 * struct ratelimit_state and __ratelimit() exist so code that builds a limiter
20 * explicitly still compiles; __ratelimit() always answers 1 (proceed).
21 */
