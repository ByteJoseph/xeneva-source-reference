Detailed Description

8250 core driver operations

@setup_irq() Setup irq handling. The universal 8250 driver links this port to the irq chain. Other drivers may @request_irq(). @release_irq() Undo irq handling. The universal 8250 driver unlinks the port from the irq chain.
