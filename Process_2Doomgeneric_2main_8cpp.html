Doomgeneric main entry point for XenevaOS.

Boots the full Doom engine (doomgeneric_Create -> D_DoomMain) with the IWAD shipped in the initrd, then pumps frames via doomgeneric_Tick(). The initrd packer places Resources/resources/* at the image root, so the WAD is visible as /doom2.wad at runtime.
