4/*
5 * DCL <linux/dma-mapping.h> -- the DMA-buffer fence markers, and nothing else.
6 *
7 * mainline's header is 27 KB: the dma_alloc_coherent family, the dmam_alloc
8 * family, dma_map_sg, dma_sync_single_*, the attrs and direction enums, the
9 * DMA_MASK checks. None of it has a caller in this tree, and all of it would
10 * need a DMA layer behind it -- which Xeneva does not have. What a ported
11 * source reaches for today is a pair of macros that fence a buffer inside a
12 * struct, so that is what this is. If a driver needs real allocation, that is
13 * the moment to decide whether the linear map is the answer and to write the
14 * rest of the header then.
15 *
16 * The two macros are mainline's, including the quirk that matters:
17 *
18 * #define __dma_from_device_group_begin(GROUP) \
19 * __cacheline_group_begin(GROUP) ____dma_from_device_aligned
20 *
21 * NO trailing semicolon (the caller's `;` terminates the member), and GROUP
22 * may be empty -- virtio-rng.c:34 and :38 write `__dma_from_device_group_begin();`
23 * with nothing inside the parens. See the note in <linux/cache.h>.
24 *
25 * ARCH_DMA_MINALIGN is not defined for this target, so
26 * ____dma_from_device_aligned is the empty spelling mainline falls back to
27 * too -- no extra alignment. That is not an oversight: virtio-rng.c guards
28 * its buffer with `#if SMP_CACHE_BYTES < 32` and sizes it itself, so the
29 * cacheline (64) already governs the layout.
30 */
31

37/*
38 * dma_alloc_coherent() / dma_free_coherent() -- the one pair the fence macros
39 * above were standing in front of.
40 *
41 * mainline's header declares a whole family (the dmam_ allocators, dma_map_sg,
42 * the direction enum, the mask checks) and this comment used to say none of it
43 * had a caller. virtio_console.c:439 is a caller:
44 *
45 * buf->buf = dma_alloc_coherent(buf->dev, buf_size, &buf->dma, GFP_KERNEL);
46 *
47 * -- the rproc-serial arm of alloc_buf(), the arm DCL folds away (see
48 * CONFIG_REMOTEPROC in <linux/autoconf.h>). It has to *compile*, and it has to
49 * link if the compiler ever declines to fold it, so the two declarations are
50 * here and the bodies are in DCL/linux_mm_shim.c.
51 *
52 * What they do: dma_alloc_coherent() is a plain kmalloc with the address
53 * handed back as the dma_addr_t as well. That is sound rather than convenient
54 * -- Xeneva's virtio path takes a *virtual* address and translates it at kick
55 * time (see <linux/scatterlist.h> on why buf is a linear-map pointer), so the
56 * only thing this tree ever does with buf->dma is hand it straight back to
57 * dma_free_coherent() at :373, and a token that round-trips is a token that
58 * works. There is no streaming mapping here to need a bus address, and no
59 * IOMMU to need an address the CPU cannot reach.
60 *
61 * The parameter types are mainline's, so a caller written against mainline
62 * compiles without a cast: `struct device *` (incomplete is fine -- nothing
63 * here dereferences it), size_t, `dma_addr_t *`, gfp_t.
64 */
